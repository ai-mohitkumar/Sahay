# The Trade-Off Engine: Negotiation Logic & Prompts

## Why Negotiation Beats Pure Scheduling

When a user misses a schedule item in standard apps:
- **Todoist / Notion**: Task remains overdue with a red tag. Guilt increases. User stops checking the app.
- **Motion / Reclaim**: Automatically shifts the task forward into tomorrow without asking. Next day has 12 hours of stacked work. User burns out and uninstalls.
- **Sahay (The AI Ally)**: Pauses, calculates consequence, and negotiates mutually agreed trades with the student.

---

## The 4 Principles of Sahay Negotiation

1. **Emotional Sticky Reality**:
   Explain the impact in tangible metrics (exam readiness %, catchup minutes needed, syllabus delay) rather than generic warnings.

2. **Always Offer 3 Viable Paths**:
   - **Path A (Same-Day Salvage)**: Move to evening buffer / swap with leisure.
   - **Path B (Painless Split)**: Break into two smaller sessions tomorrow.
   - **Path C (Weekend Offload)**: Move to Saturday morning power hour.
   - **Path D (Honest Concession)**: Drop task, take the hit to readiness %, and re-balance syllabus.

3. **No Phantom Guilt**:
   Once an agreement is accepted, the schedule is clean. The user feels relief and ownership rather than failure.

4. **Continuous Learning**:
   Every negotiation choice writes to `activity_history`. If a user always rejects evening shifts when tired, Sahay learns to stop proposing evening slots on high-fatigue days.
